How it works

From target to proof, autonomously.

Point Sentinel at a target you're authorized to test. It emulates a real attacker end to end — reasoning on-device, proving impact, and sealing the evidence. You stay in control of the scope; it does the work.

01

Recon

Maps the authorized scope — hosts, services, versions, and reachable attack surface. On-device, nothing leaves.

02

Exploit

Chains reachable services to known-exploitable weaknesses and validates them with controlled, scope-bounded execution.

03

Proof of impact

Doesn't stop at "potentially vulnerable." Captures sealed evidence of a real, scope-bounded foothold — proof, not guesses.

04

Report

Produces an evidence-linked report — every finding references the sealed artifact that proves it, ready to hand off.

Deploy

Plug it in. That's the setup.

No agent to install, no console to babysit, no cloud account to wire up. Connect power, plug the ethernet into the network you're authorized to test, and Sentinel takes it from there.

Connect power

The appliance boots straight into its engagement runtime — no imaging, no setup wizard, no OS to configure.

Plug into the network

One ethernet cable into the network you're cleared to test. No agents to deploy, no credentials to pre-stage.

Walk away

Sentinel runs the engagement end to end and seals the evidence. Come back to a report, not a task list.

The hard, grinding work is handled — you keep the part that's actually fun.

Platform

An intruder's reasoning, running on hardware you own.

Not another scanner that prints a list of maybes. Sentinel thinks in attack paths — how one exposed service becomes a validated foothold — and refuses to act outside the scope you signed.

On-device AI

A local model reasons over the evidence and chains the attack path. No cloud, no API calls, no third party in the loop.

Scope-enforced

Fail-closed by design. Anything outside the signed scope is refused before it runs. Zero out-of-scope actions, every time.

Proof-grade evidence

Every finding carries a sealed artifact. Status advances only with proof — "available" is never mistaken for "exploitable."

Attack-path reasoning

Sees the chain, not just the list — recon → exposed service → working exploit → validated impact, with a rationale at each step.

Fully offline

Egress disabled at the boundary. Your targets, your findings, and your evidence never leave the appliance.

Adversary emulation

Emulates a real attacker the way elite red teams do — and, on the roadmap, probes the perimeter itself before it ever gets inside.

The appliance

An attacker's mind, in a box you own.

Sentinel runs entirely on a compact edge appliance built on NVIDIA Jetson. Local inference, local evidence, local everything. Plug it into the network you're authorized to test and it works air-gapped — no account, no upload, no one else's servers.

Cloud pentest platforms route your most sensitive reconnaissance and findings through infrastructure you don't control. Sentinel never does. That's not a feature flag — it's the whole design.
